How this score works

The main board measures everyone against the top student, so one very big account squeezes everyone else down. This experiment measures each student against fixed targets instead - your score depends only on your own numbers, never on anyone else's.

  • Posting - target: 6 posts a week. Counts for 70%.
  • Views - target: 2 lakh views across your 12 most recent reels. Counts for 30%.

Each part is marked out of 100 against its target - reach the target and you get full marks for it. Final score = 70% posting + 30% views. Followers no longer count toward the score. The targets are set for a cohort that is about a month old: they describe a strong first month, not a celebrity account. Nobody loses points because someone else did well.
